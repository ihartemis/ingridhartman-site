- **Opens on this week** — The Schedule starts on the current week. Earlier weeks sit behind a "Show earlier weeks" control, and a Today chip brings the list back after scrolling.
- **Empty days** — A day with no care shows a dashed placeholder card, with an Add button on today and upcoming days.
- **Clearer sessions** — Your own sessions read "Your house", and kid avatars are larger so faces are legible.
- **Time steppers** — Start and end times in the add-session flow are inline 15-minute steppers, defaulting to 9 to 5.
- **Layout fixes** — The close button clears the step indicator on notched phones, and the login screen's Terms and Privacy links are tappable.
