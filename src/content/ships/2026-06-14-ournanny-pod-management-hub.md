- The Pod tab is where a share is managed now: a Manage Family screen (name, kids), a Manage Nanny screen (rates, bio), and contextual buttons to invite a nanny or add a family.
- The person who created the pod is treated as its owner everywhere, not just in billing. Before this the creator was missing the admin family switcher and the invite button on their own pod.
- The greeting-and-avatar header was removed from every screen, and profile and app settings moved to a single row at the bottom of the Pod tab.
