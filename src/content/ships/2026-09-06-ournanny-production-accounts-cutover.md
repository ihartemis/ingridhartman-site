- **Production accounts** — The app now runs on its production sign-in system. Everyone signs in once more with the same phone number and finds their pod, schedule, and billing exactly where they left them.
- **Verification codes** — Text-message codes now come from OurNanny's own pipeline, so signing in works the same whether you were invited or arrived on your own.
- **One account per number** — Signing in with a phone number that already has an account always lands on that account, never a second one.
