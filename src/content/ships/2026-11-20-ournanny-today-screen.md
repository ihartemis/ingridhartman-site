- **A storybook header** — Today opens on a hand-drawn scene of your day: the house the session is at, the kids in the windows and the nanny at the door, under a sky that follows the time of day.
- **One card for the day** — One sentence says who's with whom and until when, like "Maya is taking care of Leo & June until 5:00 PM." Sick days, time away and payday show up there too.
- **Tap a kid or the nanny** — Mark them out sick or away, or edit the session.
- **One + button** — Add a session, a sick day or time away from Today or Schedule.
- **Holidays** — Today names the day, like "Thursday · November 26 · Thanksgiving," and the header dresses up for it with pumpkins, snow and lights, fireworks, hearts or bats.
- **Who's off** — See who's away that day and when they're back, like "Maya is on vacation, paid · back Wednesday."
