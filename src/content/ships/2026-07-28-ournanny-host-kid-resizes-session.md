- Change the session by editing a host family kid's hours. Extend or shorten the kid's time and the session follows; no separate session edit needed.
- Works after a session has ended too, for the "she actually stayed until 6" corrections. Billing updates automatically.
- The sheet says what is about to happen ("Extends the session to 8:45 AM–5:15 PM") before saving, and on weekly sessions still asks whether it is this session or all future ones.
- Kids from other families are never moved by these edits.
