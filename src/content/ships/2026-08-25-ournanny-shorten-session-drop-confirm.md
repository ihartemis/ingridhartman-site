- **Shorten or move it anyway** — A session can be shortened or moved even when another family's kid is booked for time that no longer fits. The app names who is affected and asks before removing their time.
- **The other parent hears about it** — When an edit trims or removes their kid's time, that parent gets a notification linked to the session so they can rebook.
- **Same everywhere** — The confirmation appears whichever way the session is shortened: from the session time tile, from a kid's hours, or from Today.
