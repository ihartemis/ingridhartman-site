- **Paid holidays** — Pick which holidays are in your nanny's contract. On those days the regular sessions don't run and the nanny is still paid. Schedule shows the holiday by name.
- **Vacation days** — Set a number of days per year. Time away is paid from those days until they run out, and the app shows how many are left.
- **How days off are paid** — Choose one: what that day's usual sessions would have cost, with each family paying its usual part, or a flat amount split by percentages you set.
- **Working a holiday** — Set time and a half or double time for sessions worked on a holiday. Tap the holiday on Schedule to add your nanny back for the day.
- **What each family sees** — The nanny's profile and Billing say what the contract means for your family. The nanny sees days left and paid holidays.
