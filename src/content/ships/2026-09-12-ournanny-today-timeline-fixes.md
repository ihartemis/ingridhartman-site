- Today's timeline is back to its intended shape: capsules the normal width, each nanny's photo (same size as the kids') sitting on her stretch of the rail in her color, hour labels locked to the gridlines. The "Sol takes over" callout and the stray "back 1:00" note between two nannies' sessions are gone.
- Nanny colors now match across Today and Schedule. A nanny who has been set up but hasn't accepted yet was being left out of Today's count, which made every nanny teal there.
- Tapping a kid on a day with two sessions opens the session you tapped, not always the first.
- The "+" to add a session from Today shows on every day, not only today, and for the pod's organizer too.
- Schedule dates line up with the first tile on every row. Nanny photos show in the billing activity list, and the Pod page's account row shows your own photo.
