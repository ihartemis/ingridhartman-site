- **Timeline shape** — Capsules are back to their normal width, each nanny's photo sits on her stretch of the rail in her color at the same size as the kids', and hour labels lock to the gridlines.
- **Nanny colors** — A nanny's color now matches across Today and Schedule, including a nanny who has been set up but hasn't accepted yet.
- **Tapping a kid** — On a day with two sessions, tapping a kid opens the session you tapped.
- **Adding a session** — The "+" on Today shows on every day, not only today, and for the pod's organizer too.
- **Times and dates** — Each stretch of a kid's day shows its own start and end, so a morning and an afternoon read 8:45–12:15 and 1:00–5:00. Schedule dates line up with the first tile on every row.
