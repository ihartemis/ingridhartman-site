- **Payday ping** — Parents get a notification once the day's last session ends, and overdue families get a daily reminder until they pay. Tapping it opens Billing.
- **The nanny's side** — The nanny hears when families have been reminded, and again when everyone is paid up, with the on-time streak included.
- **Quiet by default** — Families who are paid up hear nothing, quiet weeks send nothing, and nothing goes out outside 9 to 9.
- **Payment receipt** — Recording a payment ends in a receipt: what the week cost, what the share saved, and the streak, with confetti.
- **New build** — Push notifications need TestFlight build 84.
