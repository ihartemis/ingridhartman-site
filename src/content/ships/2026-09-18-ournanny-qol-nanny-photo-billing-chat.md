- **Nanny photos** — A nanny can add or change her profile photo any time from the About sheet on her profile, and pod admins can set it from the same place, the way they already can for kids and family members.
- **Bigger faces on Today** — Kid avatars in the day capsules, the nanny's photo on the timeline rail, and the small face in the "who's here" chip are all larger. The layout is unchanged.
- **Billing estimate** — The estimated amount at the top of Billing renders in full while a session is still running.
- **Empty days** — On a day with no sessions, Today's card offers "Add a session" directly. The "+" in the header appears only on days that already have one.
- **Session chat** — Session chat is limited to a single pilot pod. Other pods don't see the chat dock, can't open a thread, and get no chat notifications.
