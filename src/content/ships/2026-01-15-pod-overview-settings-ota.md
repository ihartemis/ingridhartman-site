- **Pod overview** — The Pod tab shows a card for each family with its children, and a card for the nanny.
- **Settings** — A Settings screen with a light, dark, or system theme choice.
- **Pods without a nanny** — Adding a session works in a pod that has no nanny assigned yet.
- **Updates without reinstalling** — Improvements now reach an installed copy of the app on its next launch, so most changes arrive without a fresh download.
