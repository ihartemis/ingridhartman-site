- **Split windows** — A kid's attendance can be split into up to three windows in one session, morning and afternoon around a nap or a class, without creating two sessions. The kid's card shows the windows as stacked lines.
- **One tap to split** — "+ Add another time" is always reachable, and a full-day window splits around a one-hour break in a single tap.
- **Repeats too** — Split windows repeat with a weekly series, and the "this session only / this and future" question applies.
- **Cost follows** — Cost is priced slice by slice, so the host's share goes up correctly when another kid is out mid-day.
