- A kid's attendance can be split into up to three windows in one session, morning and afternoon around a nap or a class, without creating two sessions. The kid's card shows the windows as stacked lines.
- "+ Add another time" is always reachable: with a full-day window it splits the day around a one-hour break in one tap.
- Split windows repeat with a weekly series, and the "this session only / this and future" question applies.
- Billing needed no changes. The cost engine already priced time slice by slice, so the host's share correctly goes up when another kid is out mid-day.
