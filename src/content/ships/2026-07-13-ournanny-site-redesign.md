- **Current copy** — Pre-launch lines are gone. The page now says it is welcoming early families, and free for them. The word beta does not appear.
- **Share previews** — A link posted anywhere shows the right preview image, served from the site itself.
- **Phone first** — The page is tighter on a phone, with the call to action above the fold, and links Terms and Privacy from the footer.
