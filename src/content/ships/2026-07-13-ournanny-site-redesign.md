- ournanny.app was rebuilt as a warm editorial page on the app's own palette and fonts: a left-aligned hero with a slot for a demo video, a ribbon of the chores the app takes off a parent's plate, a features grid with a live rate-tier example, and a mission section.
- The hero now reads "Having a nanny, minus the spreadsheet", and the subhead covers a single family with a nanny as well as a nanny share.
- Stale pre-launch copy is gone. "Launching early 2026" and the "only 100 spots" line were replaced with "now welcoming early families" and "free for early families". The word beta does not appear on the page.
- Social previews work again: the share image had pointed at a file that did not exist, so links posted anywhere showed nothing. Images moved from a third-party host into the site itself.
- The page went from about 1,600 lines to 600, is tighter on a phone (call to action above the fold at 375px), and links Terms and Privacy from the footer.
