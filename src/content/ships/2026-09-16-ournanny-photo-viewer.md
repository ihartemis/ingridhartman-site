- Opening a photo in a session chat now shows a clear close button and a share button on dark pills, below the status bar. Before, the close button was drawn on top of the clock and battery, in white, with nothing behind it — invisible on a bright photo.
- Tapping the photo itself closes the viewer. One beta tester had to force-quit the app to get out; that is the report this fixes.
