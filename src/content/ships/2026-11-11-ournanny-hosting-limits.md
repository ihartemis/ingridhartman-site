- **Hosting limits** — The number you set for your place counts other families' kids only. Your own kids never count, so it doesn't matter which of them come on a given day.
- **When a session can't fit** — If a weekly session can't fit on any date, the app says why and which limit to raise, and nothing half-saved is left behind.
- **Skipped dates** — When only some dates fit, the confirmation says how many were skipped. Raise your place's limit and those dates fill in.
- **Choosing a nanny** — In a share with more than one nanny, tapping Add to schedule before picking one tells you to choose.
