- Sign-in is by phone number. Enter the number, type the code from the text, and you are in. No passwords, and the Apple and Google buttons are gone.
- Invites go by phone too. An admin enters a name and number and gets an invite code plus a pre-written text to send; the invitee's account connects to that invite when they sign up with the same number.
- The login screens were redesigned around a single logo on a warm ivory background.
- Under the hood, accounts created by phone work everywhere an email account did, so nothing else in the app had to change.
