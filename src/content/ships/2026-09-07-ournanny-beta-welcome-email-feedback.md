- **Welcome screen** — A short welcome from the founder on first sign-up, saying what the beta is and who built it.
- **Email on your profile** — Email is now part of every profile, for payday reminders and schedule changes, not marketing. It stays a contact, not a login. New users enter it during setup; existing users are asked once on the Pod tab.
- **Feedback button** — On Today and in the Pod tab: describe the problem, attach a screenshot, and it goes to the team.
- **Settings together** — Account, notifications, appearance, and What's new now live together on the Pod tab.
