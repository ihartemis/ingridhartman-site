- A short welcome from the founder on first sign-up. The first strangers from a Reddit ad had reached a beta that never said it was a beta or who built it.
- Email is now part of every profile, for payday reminders and schedule changes, not marketing. It stays a contact, not a login. New users enter it during setup; existing users are asked once on the Pod tab.
- A Feedback button on Today and in the Pod tab: describe the problem, attach a screenshot, and it goes to the team. Before this there was no way to report anything from inside the app.
- Account, notifications, appearance, and What's new now live together on the Pod tab.
