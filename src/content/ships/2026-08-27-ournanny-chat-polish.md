- **Familiar chat** — Bigger text, faces on messages, timestamps tucked inside the bubble, a double tick that turns teal once everyone has read it, tapback reactions with haptics, and "joined the session" lines.
- **Read receipts** — Swipe left on one of your own messages to see who has read it and when. Swipe right anywhere to go back.
- **Card and header** — On Today the chat card has a larger face, a two-line preview, and a deeper shadow. The thread header groups the crew by family instead of listing individuals.
- **Notification controls** — Session-chat notifications can be turned off for yourself in Pod settings, and if the phone's notifications are off the app says so and links to Settings.
- **Fixes** — Swiping the thread sideways is stable, and the text field stays put when sending photos.
