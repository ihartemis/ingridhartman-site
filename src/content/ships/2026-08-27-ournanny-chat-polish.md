- Chat now works like the messengers people already use: bigger text, faces on messages, timestamps tucked inside the bubble with a double tick that turns teal once everyone has read it, tapback reactions with haptics, and "joined the session" lines so it is clear who is in the room.
- Swipe left on one of your own messages to see exactly who has read it and when. Swipe right anywhere to go back.
- The chat card on Today got bigger: a larger face, a two-line preview, and a deeper shadow so it reads as a door.
- The thread header lists the crew grouped by family rather than as a run of individual names.
- In Pod settings, session-chat notifications can be turned off for yourself without leaving the thread. If system notifications are off, the app now says so with a full-screen prompt that links to Settings, since nothing had told testers before.
- Fixed: a crash when sliding the whole thread sideways, and the text field disappearing when sending photos.
