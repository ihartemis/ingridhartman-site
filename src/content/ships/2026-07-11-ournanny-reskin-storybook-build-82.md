- **Brand look** — The app carries the website's warm cream, ink and sand palette, the brand typefaces, and a deeper teal.
- **Everything responds** — Taps get a small spring and a light haptic tick, the tab pill animates, screens transition, and recording a payment sets off confetti.
- **Tab accents** — Each tab has its own accent in the tab bar: Schedule teal, Billing amber, Pod coral. The Share tab is called Pod again.
- **Empty screens** — An empty screen explains what belongs there instead of showing nothing.
- **New build** — Fonts and haptics are native pieces, so this ships as a new TestFlight build (82).
