- Fixed: a repeating session generated by the nightly job could re-add a kid whose family was away, which would have billed that family for a session the kid did not attend. New sessions are now born with away kids already marked out, and "this and future" edits no longer put them back.
- An away nanny is no longer auto-assigned to new sessions.
- One affected session in the live beta was repaired before the next payday.
