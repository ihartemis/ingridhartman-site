- The app opens on a new Today tab: a living timeline of today's session that fills in as the day happens, with a capsule per kid and a teal rail for the nanny's span. Who has a kid right now, until when, and at whose house, on one screen.
- Chips are doors. The house chip opens a map with a copyable address; the crew chip shows who is there, with ages.
- Any other day is a plan view of that day's session, and quiet days show what is next for each kid. Past days show fully filled in.
- Editing a kid's hours from Today follows the same session-resize rules as the session details.
- The unfinished Feed tab is gone; the app has four tabs.
