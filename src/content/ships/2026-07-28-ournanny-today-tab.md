- **Today** — The app opens on a living timeline of today's session, with a capsule per kid and a teal rail for the nanny's span. Who has a kid right now, until when, and at whose house, on one screen.
- **Chips are doors** — The house chip opens a map with a copyable address; the crew chip shows who is there, with ages.
- **Any other day** — Other days show a plan view of that day's session, and quiet days show what is next for each kid.
- **Edit from Today** — Changing a kid's hours here follows the same rules as editing the session itself.
- **Four tabs** — The unfinished Feed tab is gone.
