- Sign-up starts with a question about the family's situation, not a role: there is a nanny share to set up, an invite to one, or a wish to be matched. Each answer leads to its own short path.
- Setting up a share is set-up-then-invite: build the family, set up the nanny, add another family, and text each invite from a hub that shows what is done and what is left. The share gets a name, with a suggestion taken from the street.
- Families are named after their lead parent ("Maya's family") instead of a typed name nobody in a share uses. Kid birthdays are optional and ages show in words; birthdates are never displayed.
- Forms follow one rule: a bottom sheet for a small edit like a person or a kid, a full screen for anything with address search or more than a few fields.
- Rates are whole dollars, Venmo handles get the @ added, and payment frequency is weekly, which is how billing works.
