- **Estimate for the week** — Billing shows where the week is heading with "Est. total by Friday ≈ $X" on both the parent and nanny homes, and a sheet that prices each remaining session.
- **Payday-aware weeks** — The billing week ends on the nanny's actual payday, and every due date and label follows it.
- **Pay in Venmo** — Venmo opens prefilled with a personal memo ("Taking care of Theo · Jul 9–15"). On return the app asks whether it went through, and a banner catches a hand-off that was never confirmed.
- **The nanny can record** — The nanny can record a payment herself, choosing which family it came from.
- **Billed on open** — Sessions that have ended are billed when Billing is opened, so the amount to pay is ready.
