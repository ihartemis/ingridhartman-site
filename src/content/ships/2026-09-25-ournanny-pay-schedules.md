- **Pay schedules** — Pay a nanny every week, every other week, twice a month or once a month. The schedule is written the way you'd say it: "Every other Friday", "The 15th and 30th".
- **Picking a payday** — Choose her next payday on a calendar, then how it repeats. The next three paydays show before you save, including what February does.
- **Switching schedules** — Pay periods you've already paid stay exactly as they were. Anything not yet paid moves to the new schedule and is due on the new first payday.
- **Billing follows it** — Billing, payday reminders and receipts follow each nanny's own schedule, with dates like "Sep 16 – 30" when she isn't paid weekly. A progress bar shows how many sessions are done.
- **From the start** — Setting up a nanny asks for her schedule, and it's what she sees when she accepts your invite.
