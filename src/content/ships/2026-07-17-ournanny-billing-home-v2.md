- One money card that changes with the week: ink while charges accrue, marigold on payday with a prefilled "Pay $X in Venmo", red only when something is overdue, green when paid up. Color tracks urgency, never the mere existence of a number.
- Overdue pays exactly the late amount, attributed oldest-first, with a "Catching up" memo. This week stays a separate quiet row.
- Tap the card for the week sheet: each completed session with its math, scheduled sessions at their estimate, carryover, savings, and a "Pay early" link.
- The nanny's home mirrors it: earning, payday, or behind, with a live settle list on payday that ticks families off as they pay.
- Record and adjust live in sheets. Zelle joins Venmo as a payment method; adjustments need a written reason, and the nanny can make them too.
- A quiet streak line appears under the paid-up card after three on-time weeks, and hero amounts count up when they change.
