- If you were set up by your pod's organizer and signed in with the invited number, the app now recognizes your invite no matter what email you type during setup. Before, entering a contact email could make the app lose track of the invite and finish setup with you outside the pod.
- As a backstop, finishing setup now joins you to any pod that was still waiting on you, and a failed join shows an error instead of quietly completing.
