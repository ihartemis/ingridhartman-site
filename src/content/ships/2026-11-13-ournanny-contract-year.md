- **Contract year** — Tap the year beside Paid days off on your nanny's profile to pick the month it starts in. Paid time off and sick days start over on the 1st of that month.
- **What follows it** — The holiday count and the extra day a weekend holiday adds count by the same year. Pay that already went out stays as it is.
- **Joining a share** — A parent who joins a share where the nanny already has paid days off gets a note on what that means for their family.
- **Smaller things** — "Working a holiday" reads Not set until someone answers it. Today names a holiday that is paid on a different day as observed.
