- **Contract year** — Tap the year beside Paid days off on your nanny's profile to pick the month it starts in. Paid time off, sick days and the holiday count follow it. Pay that already went out stays as it is.
- **Kids missing from a repeat** — When a kid is on every recent session of a weekday but not on the repeat, the profile offers to add the kid, so a day off is priced with everyone.
- **Setting up a nanny** — Setup asks one question: do you have a contract? Say yes and adding it joins your setup list after your first session.
- **Joining a share** — A parent joining a share where the nanny has paid days off gets a note on what it means for their family.
- **Smaller things** — "Working a holiday" reads Not set until someone answers it. Today names a holiday paid on a different day as observed.
