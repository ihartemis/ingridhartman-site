- **Host kids' hours** — A kid from the host family can be on a session for part of it. The host family still needs at least one kid on the session.
- **Moving a session** — Move a session to another family's house and that family's kids join for the whole session. The location sheet says so before you save, and you can change their hours after.
- **Schedule messages** — When the schedule can't save a change, the app says why.
