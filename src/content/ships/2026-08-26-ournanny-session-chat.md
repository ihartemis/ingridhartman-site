- Every session now has its own chat for the crew: the host family, joining families, and the nanny. Nobody else can see it. The privacy rule is enforced on the server and covered by a test, not only hidden in the app.
- Photos go straight into the thread, up to four at a time. Tap one to view it full-screen and save it from the share sheet.
- Notifications are quiet until about an hour before a kid's drop-off, then every message comes through, including the after-pickup photos. Any session's thread can be muted.
- The thread stays open through the day after the session for thank-yous and lost jackets, then becomes read-only and stays readable.
- Today shows a one-line preview of the current session's chat above the timeline.
