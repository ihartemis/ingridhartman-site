- **A thread per session** — Every session has its own chat for the crew: the host family, joining families, and the nanny. Nobody else can see it.
- **Photos** — Send up to four at a time. Tap one to view it full-screen and save it from the share sheet.
- **Quiet until it matters** — Notifications stay off until about an hour before a kid's drop-off, then every message comes through. Any thread can be muted.
- **Open through the next day** — The thread stays open the day after the session for thank-yous and lost jackets, then becomes read-only and stays readable.
- **On Today** — A one-line preview of the current session's chat sits above the timeline.
