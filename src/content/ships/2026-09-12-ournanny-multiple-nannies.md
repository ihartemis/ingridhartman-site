- **More than one nanny** — Under the Pod tab's nanny section there's a "+ Add another nanny". Each nanny gets her own profile, rates, and payday, and can be scheduled before she ever logs in.
- **Scheduling a nanny** — With two or more nannies, creating a session asks which one is working it, with live availability. Two nannies can run at once; the same nanny can't.
- **Billing per nanny** — One card for the week across all nannies, with a row per nanny to pay each separately. The activity list names the nanny on every line.
- **What nannies see** — Nannies see each other's names, photos, and bios, and that a colleague's session exists — never her rates, payday, or session details.
- **Today with a handoff** — Back-to-back sessions at the same house merge into one day view, with the rail changing color at the handoff and each nanny's photo on her stretch.
