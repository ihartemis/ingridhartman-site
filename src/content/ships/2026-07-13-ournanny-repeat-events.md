- **Repeat weekly** — A session can repeat weekly. Pick the days and optionally an end date; each weekday becomes its own series, so Wednesday can carry a different kid list from Monday.
- **Three-step wizard** — Adding a session is three steps: date and time with a week strip and repeat control, then where, then kids. Landing on the schedule with the new sessions pulsing is the review.
- **Editing a series** — Editing a repeating session asks "this session only" or "this and future sessions". Past sessions feed billing, and a series edit leaves them alone.
- **Convert and navigate** — A one-time session can become weekly from its details, and the week strip keeps today marked, with a "Today" chip when paged away.
- **Time away respected** — An away family's house cannot host, and an away kid cannot be added.
