- **Start a share** — Lay out your nanny, your family, the family you share with and your week before you make an account. See what each family pays and when your nanny's first payday is, then save it.
- **Joining by invite** — Invited parents and nannies see the share's week and what it means for them before they join. Whoever runs the share and its nanny get a notification when someone joins.
- **Finish setting up** — A checklist on Today shows what's left, like invites, your address and payday, and checks each one off as it's done.
- **Your contract** — A nanny can now fill in and change her own contract.
- **Your email** — Everyone gives an email for account updates. If you're already in the app, it asks once.
