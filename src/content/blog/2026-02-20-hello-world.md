I built this site at a ShipSLC session on a Friday morning. Two hours, a cup of coffee, and a determination to stop overthinking it.

I've wanted a personal site for a while — not a portfolio, not a "thought leadership platform," not a newsletter. Just a place to write about whatever I'm thinking about. Product work, parenthood, building things, random observations. No theme. No content calendar. Just writing.

If you're here, you probably came from one of my projects or found me on X. Welcome. Poke around. The old man by the fire doesn't bite.
